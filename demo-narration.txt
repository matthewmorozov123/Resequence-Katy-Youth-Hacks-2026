Hello, this is Matthew and I would like to show you Resequence.

Resequence is an evidence-aware coach that turns a real day into personalized guidance instead of generic productivity rules.

On the first visit, users enter their name, choose their main productivity challenge, and describe what they want to change. This gives the analysis a personal target instead of a generic routine.

Then, users choose today's priorities, rate their importance and difficulty, and can load a sample day with one click.

The timeline records what actually happened. Quick Capture turns a natural sentence with a time and duration into a timeline entry.

Once it is there, the activity can be renamed. Its duration can be changed. And it can be moved. It can also be connected to a priority.

In Outcomes, users report their progress. Partial completion counts, producing a more honest result than a simple checklist.

Before the AI analysis, users open the evidence library. Each source shows its supported claims and limitations and can be turned on or off. The AI uses only enabled sources.

Finally, Resequence combines the timeline, outcomes, personal challenge, and enabled research. The debrief explains what worked, identifies possible friction, and recommends one small experiment.

It then gives three flexible suggestions for tomorrow, each with an anchor, success measure, and evidence. It does not invent a schedule or claim that one day proves causation.

The result is personalized, transparent advice that helps users make a better next decision. That is Resequence: learn from today, and improve tomorrow.
